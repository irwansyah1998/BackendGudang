## Safety-First Walk

Record, do not relocate or alter conditions during survey unless the authorized site safety owner directs an immediate risk response.

| Observation ID | Area / temporary ID | Observation | Evidence/photo ref | Immediate danger? | Access/people/equipment affected | Action/escalation owner | Closed/verified by |

Check: emergency exits and routes, fire equipment, electrical panels, pedestrian/forklift crossings, damaged racks, unstable or leaning loads, falling-object exposure, spills/leaks, floor damage, lighting, ventilation, overhead obstructions, incompatible materials, and manual handling risks. A qualified safety/engineering owner decides applicable controls and clearances.
