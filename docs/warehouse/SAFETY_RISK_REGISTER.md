## Immediate Danger Response

If a direct imminent danger is observed, do not continue the survey in the affected area or attempt unqualified corrective work. Follow the site's established emergency response, notify the authorized safety/site lead immediately, and keep people away as directed. This document is not an emergency procedure and supplies no site-specific contacts.

## Approval Gate

Before any physical layout change, label/zone release, capacity claim, stack limit, aisle marking, equipment route, rack use, or digital availability status is approved, obtain the responsible site's documented review and any required qualified technical/safety sign-off. Record the applicable jurisdictional requirement and evidence source; none has been confirmed here.
